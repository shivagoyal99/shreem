LIRA

A Locked-In Retirement Account holds pension money from a former employer.

What it is

When you leave an employer, you may be able to move your pension into a LIRA. It gives you control over how the money is invested. It stays locked in until retirement.

Main benefits

  • Investments grow without tax inside the account
  • You choose the investments
  • At retirement, it can be converted to a Life Income Fund (LIF)
