About us

Holistic financial planning that puts you in control of your goals.

Plan well so you can live well

Shreem Financial Group helps Canadians plan around their goals and needs, from protecting their family and their income to saving for retirement, education and a home. Our name carries our tagline: your way to a FIRE journey, which means financial independence and retiring early.

We work with a range of Canadian insurers, so we can compare coverage and cost instead of offering a single product. Our advice is based on your situation, in plain language, with no pressure to buy.

What we help with

  • Life, health, disability, critical illness and travel insurance
  • Group benefits and financial planning for business owners
  • RRSP, TFSA, RESP, retirement income and investment funds
  • Mortgages for purchase, renewal and refinance
  • Residential and commercial real estate through Pontis Realty
