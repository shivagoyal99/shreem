Common questions

Do I need a real estate lawyer?

An Ontario real estate lawyer handles the legal work for closing, including title and the transfer of funds. We coordinate with your lawyer. We work alongside yours.

How is a REALTOR paid?

Usually through a commission agreed in writing before you sign, and often paid from the sale. Ask us to explain it before you decide.

Should I sell first or buy first?

It depends on your finances, the market and how flexible your dates are. We map out both paths with your mortgage so you can choose.

How much does it cost to buy?

Besides the down payment, plan for legal fees, Ontario land transfer tax, inspection or appraisal, and moving costs.

What should I know before buying a pre-construction home?

Read the agreement of purchase and sale carefully, understand the deposit schedule and closing costs, and have your lawyer review it. Closing dates can move, so keep your mortgage approval and financing flexible.

How long does a sale take?

It depends on price, condition, season and location. We give you a realistic range after reviewing your property.
