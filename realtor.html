Real Estate

Buying or selling property in Mississauga and the GTA, with the financing planned from day one.

Buying a home

  • Plan. We start with your budget, your mortgage pre-approval and what matters most in a home.
  • Search. We find properties that fit and arrange viewings.
  • Offer. We prepare the offer and negotiate on your behalf.
  • Close. Your real estate lawyer handles the legal side, and we coordinate the financing and the closing date.

Selling your property

  • Price it. We compare recent sales and current listings in your area.
  • Prepare it. Practical advice on what to fix, clean or stage before photos.
  • Market it. Listing, photography, showings and offers.
  • Plan the next step. We look at what the sale means for your next purchase and your mortgage.

Common questions

Do I need a real estate lawyer?

In Ontario, a lawyer or licensed paralegal handles the legal side of closing, including title and funds. We work alongside yours.

How is a REALTOR paid?

Usually through a commission agreed in writing before you sign, and often paid from the sale. Ask us to explain it before you decide.

Should I sell first or buy first?

It depends on your finances, the market and how flexible your dates are. We map out both paths with your mortgage so you can choose.

How much does it cost to buy?

Besides the down payment, plan for legal fees, Ontario land transfer tax, inspection or appraisal, and moving costs.

How long does a sale take?

It depends on price, condition, season and location. We give you a realistic range after reviewing your property.
