Life Insurance

Life insurance pays a benefit to the people you choose, so your family can keep their home and their plans.

Why people buy life insurance

  • To pay off a mortgage, car loan or other debts
  • To replace your income for your family
  • To fund your children's education
  • To cover final expenses
  • To keep a business going if an owner passes away
  • To add to the cover you have through work, which usually ends when you leave your job

The benefit is generally paid to your beneficiary free of income tax.

Term life insurance

Term insurance covers you for a set number of years at a lower cost. It suits needs that end at a known time, such as the years left on a mortgage or until your children are independent.

Permanent life insurance

Permanent insurance is designed to last your whole life. It can support estate planning, leaving a legacy and covering taxes owed at death.
