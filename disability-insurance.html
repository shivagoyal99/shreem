Disability Insurance

Disability insurance pays you a monthly income if illness or injury stops you from working.

Why it matters

Your ability to earn is your biggest financial asset. If you work for a large employer you may have some group cover, but it varies a lot. If you are self-employed or work for a small company, you may have none.

What to compare

  • Own occupation or any occupation. Own occupation pays if you cannot do your regular job. Any occupation pays only if you cannot do any job.
  • Waiting period. How long you wait before payments begin.
  • Benefit period. How long payments last.
  • Monthly benefit. How much income is replaced.

How we help

We review any cover you already have, find the gap and compare policies from several insurers.
