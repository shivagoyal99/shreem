Travel Insurance

Travel with peace of mind by covering the costs your provincial plan may not.

Why you need it

Your provincial health plan may pay little or nothing for care outside Canada, and hospitals abroad can ask for payment up front. A single emergency can cost far more than a policy.

What it can cover

  • Emergency medical care and hospital stays
  • Trip cancellation and interruption
  • Lost or delayed baggage
  • Return travel if you need to come home

Choosing a plan

Single-trip plans suit one holiday. Annual multi-trip plans suit frequent travellers. If you have a medical condition, tell the insurer when you apply, because coverage for existing conditions depends on the policy.

Plans for different travellers

  • Single-trip for one holiday or family visit
  • Annual multi-trip for people who travel several times a year
  • Snowbird and long-stay plans for winter stays abroad
  • Visitors to Canada and Super Visa plans for family members visiting you

Who it is for

  • Families travelling abroad
  • Frequent business travellers
  • Seniors and snowbirds
  • Students studying abroad
  • Relatives visiting Canada

How we help

  1. We listen. We start with your family, income, debts and goals.
  2. We compare. We look at options from our insurer partners and explain the differences in plain language.
  3. We set it up and keep it current. We help with the application, then review your cover as your life changes.

Common questions

Does my provincial health plan cover me abroad?

Provincial plans usually pay only a small part of emergency costs outside Canada, and often only at set daily amounts. Travel insurance covers the rest.

Is a pre-existing medical condition covered?

It depends on the plan and on whether your condition has been stable. Always answer the medical questions fully. We explain the rules before you buy.

When should I buy it?

As soon as you book. Trip cancellation cover often starts when you buy the policy, and many plans require you to buy before departure.

Do credit cards include it?

Some do, with limits on age, trip length and amounts. Check the details and compare them with a dedicated plan.
