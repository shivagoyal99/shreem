TFSA

A Tax-Free Savings Account lets your money grow tax-free for any goal.

Main benefits

  • Growth and withdrawals are generally tax-free
  • Flexible: use it for a home, a trip, an emergency fund or retirement
  • Withdrawn amounts can be added back to your room in a later year
  • Available to Canadian residents aged 18 and over

Good to know

A TFSA is an account, not an investment. You choose what to hold inside it, such as savings, GICs or funds. Contributions are not tax-deductible, and going over your contribution room can lead to a tax. Check your room with the CRA before you contribute.

How we help

We help you pick investments inside your TFSA that match your goals and timeline.
