The role

You are driven, curious and comfortable talking about money with people from all backgrounds. You like variety, you can manage your own time and you want a career where your effort shows in your results.

What you will do

  • Meet clients to understand their goals
  • Recommend insurance, investment and mortgage solutions that fit
  • Build long-term relationships and review plans as life changes

Skills we look for

  • Good communication and a genuine interest in helping people
  • Sales and networking ability
  • Strong ethics and respect for client privacy
  • Languages besides English are an asset

The basics

  • Entry level to experienced advisors welcome
  • A life insurance licence, or the willingness to obtain one
  • Flexible hours, in Mississauga, with clients or online
  • Compensation to be discussed
