Savings and Investments

Saving for the goals that matter, from your first home to retirement.

Why save

A written plan helps you reach short, medium and long-term goals: a trip, a home, your children's education and a comfortable retirement.

Registered plans

Registered plans are accounts with special tax treatment. We help you choose and combine them.

  • RRSP: save for retirement and reduce tax today
  • TFSA: tax-free growth for any goal
  • RESP: save for a child's education with government grants
  • LIRA: keep a former employer pension growing
  • IPP: a pension plan for business owners and executives

Retirement income and funds

When you are ready to draw income, see retirement income options. To learn about the ways your money can be invested, see investment funds.

Non-registered savings

A non-registered plan lets you save more once your registered accounts are full. There is no contribution limit, and growth is taxed outside the plan.
