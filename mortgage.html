Purchase, renewal and refinance

Purchase

We work out what you can afford and what the monthly payment looks like, then shop the market for the best fit. A pre-approval shows your budget and holds a rate for a set period, often 90 to 120 days. First-time buyers should ask about the FHSA, the RRSP Home Buyers' Plan and possible land transfer tax rebates.

Renewal

Your lender will usually send an offer well before your term ends, and the first offer is not always the best. At renewal you can often move to another lender without a prepayment penalty. We compare your offer with the market so you know whether to stay or switch. Our blog has five questions to ask before you renew.

Refinance

Refinancing replaces your current mortgage with a new one, to use home equity, consolidate higher-interest debt, or change your term or rate. We show the full cost, including any penalty on your current mortgage, before you decide.

Fixed or variable?

  • Fixed rate: your rate and payment stay the same for the term, which makes budgeting simple.
  • Variable rate: the rate moves with the lender's prime rate, so your payment or the share going to interest can change.
  • The right choice depends on how much change in payments you can handle and how long you plan to keep the mortgage.

What you will need

  • Government photo ID
  • Proof of income, such as pay stubs, a letter of employment or your last two years of Notices of Assessment if you are self-employed
  • Proof of your down payment
  • Details of the property and, for a renewal, your current mortgage statement

Costs to plan for besides the down payment

  • Legal fees and title insurance
  • Ontario land transfer tax
  • Appraisal or inspection costs
  • Home insurance, property tax adjustments and moving costs

How we help

  1. We talk. Your income, savings, goals and timeline.
  2. We compare. Rates, terms and features from different lenders, with the full cost of each shown.
  3. We apply and follow through. We help gather documents, submit the application and stay with you until closing or renewal.

Common questions

How much down payment do I need?

The minimum depends on the purchase price. Putting down less than 20% generally means paying for mortgage default insurance, which is added to your mortgage.

What is the mortgage stress test?

Lenders check that you could still afford the payments at a higher qualifying rate than the one you will actually pay. It is built into pre-approval, so we plan around it.

What happens if I leave my mortgage before the term ends?

You may owe a prepayment penalty, and the amount depends on your lender and rate type. We show the formula before you sign.

Can I switch lenders at renewal?

Often yes, usually without a prepayment penalty, though there may be legal or admin costs. Check your own mortgage terms.

Can I take my mortgage with me if I move?

Many mortgages are portable, which lets you move the rate and balance to a new home within certain limits. Ask us before you sign.

General information only, not personal, financial or legal advice. Rates, rules and eligibility change and depend on the lender and your situation. Speak with an advisor, and a lawyer where relevant, before you decide.
