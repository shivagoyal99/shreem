Health Insurance

Personal health coverage fills the gaps that provincial plans leave open.

What it can cover

  • Prescription drugs
  • Dental care
  • Vision care
  • Paramedical services such as physiotherapy
  • Medical equipment and aids

Who it helps

Self-employed people, business owners, contractors, early retirees and anyone without workplace benefits. If you are self-employed, ask your accountant whether you can claim part of the premiums.

How we help

We match the plan to your family's needs and budget, so you do not pay for cover you will not use.
