Non-medical Insurance

Life insurance with no medical exam, so you can be protected quickly.

How it works

You answer health questions instead of taking a medical exam or blood test. Approval can be fast, in some cases within days.

When to consider it

  • You have health conditions that make standard cover hard to get
  • You work in a higher-risk job or play extreme sports
  • You prefer not to have exams or needles
  • You want cover in place quickly

Keep in mind

Non-medical plans often cost more for the same amount of cover. We can compare it with standard plans so you see the difference.

Who it is for

  • People who want cover without an exam
  • Applicants with health conditions
  • People who want a quick approval
  • Applicants who prefer not to give blood or urine samples
  • Those declined for standard cover who still need protection

How we help

  1. We listen. We start with your family, income, debts and goals.
  2. We compare. We look at options from our insurer partners and explain the differences in plain language.
  3. We set it up and keep it current. We help with the application, then review your cover as your life changes.

Common questions

Is it guaranteed?

Not always. Some plans ask a few health questions, and some are guaranteed issue with no questions. Guaranteed plans usually have lower limits and a waiting period.

Why does it cost more?

The insurer takes on more uncertainty because it has less medical information, so the price for the same amount of cover is usually higher.

How much can I get?

Maximums are usually lower than standard plans. The amount depends on your age, the plan and the insurer.

Can I switch to a standard policy later?

Often yes, if your health improves. We can review it with you in a few years.
