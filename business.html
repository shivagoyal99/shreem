Business

Protect your company, your team and your future.

Plans for owners and their teams

A business has more to protect than a household does: the owners, the employees, the cash flow and the value of the company itself. We help you cover each of these, and we work alongside your accountant and lawyer so the pieces fit together.

Group Benefits

A group plan pays for costs that provincial plans do not, such as dental, vision and prescription drugs, and can include life, disability and critical illness cover. We design it around your team and budget.

Group benefits in detail

Tax and Insurance Planning

Planning with your accountant helps your business keep more of what it earns. We cover key person insurance, buy-sell funding, loan protection and estate planning for owners.

Tax and insurance planning in detail

Corporate Investments

Cash left idle in the company earns little. We help you invest earnings for a purpose, such as expansion, succession or retirement, with the tax side coordinated with your accountant.

Corporate investments in detail

Also for incorporated owners

An Individual Pension Plan can build retirement savings through your company, and a personal disability or critical illness policy protects your own income.

General information only, not tax or legal advice. Speak with your accountant and lawyer before you decide.
