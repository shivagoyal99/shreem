Super Visa Insurance

The medical insurance parents and grandparents need to visit for up to five years at a time.

Get Super Visa cover

The basics

The Super Visa lets parents and grandparents of Canadian citizens and permanent residents stay in Canada for up to five years at a time. The visa can be valid for up to ten years. Visitors on a Super Visa cannot work in Canada.

What the insurance must have

  • At least $100,000 of emergency medical cover
  • Issued by a Canadian insurance company
  • Valid for at least one year from the date of entry
  • Proof of the policy to attach to the application

What is usually covered

  • Emergency hospital stays and surgery
  • Doctor visits and specialist referrals
  • Ambulance
  • Emergency prescription drugs
  • Dental emergencies
  • Medical evacuation and return home

Plan options

Some plans cover stable pre-existing conditions, and each insurer defines “stable” differently. A higher deductible can lower the premium, and many insurers allow monthly payments. Tell us about any health conditions up front so we can match you with the right plan.

Who it is for

  • Parents and grandparents applying for a Super Visa
  • Canadian families sponsoring a parent
  • Visitors who need proof of insurance for the application
  • Families who want to spread the cost with monthly payments
  • Anyone with a stable health condition who needs the right plan

How we help

  1. We listen. We start with your family, income, debts and goals.
  2. We compare. We look at options from our insurer partners and explain the differences in plain language.
  3. We set it up and keep it current. We help with the application, then review your cover as your life changes.

Common questions

Should I buy before the visa is approved?

Many families do, because proof of insurance is part of the application. Many insurers refund the premium if the visa is refused, but check the terms first.

Are existing conditions covered?

Some plans cover stable conditions. The definition of stable varies, so we compare plans for you.

What if my parent goes home early?

Most policies refund the unused portion if no claim was made. Terms vary by insurer.

Can the premium be paid monthly?

Several insurers allow it. We will show you the options.

Not sure what fits?

Tell us a little about your situation and an advisor will come back to you with options.
